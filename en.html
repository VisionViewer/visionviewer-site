Two ways to integrate · one tag, one call

Your seat map stays as it is. You add a button: the 3D view opens full screen, from the seats the moviegoer has just picked.

  1. Step 1They pick their seats on your seat map.
  2. Step 2They open the 3D view and move their seats around to compare.
  3. Step 3They return to your seat map with their choice, and carry on with their order.

You set aside a block in your booking page. Our seat map is inserted there: the moviegoer picks their seats in it, then opens the 3D view.

  1. Step 1Our seat map shows in your page, in a light or a dark theme.
  2. Step 2They pick up to nine seats in it, then compare them in 3D.
  3. Step 3Your page receives their choice, and carries on with the order.

Frequently asked questions

The questions a cinema asks.

VisionViewer is a tool for cinemas: it shows every moviegoer the view from their seat, in 3D, during online booking and before payment. Each auditorium is rebuilt to its real dimensions: rows, slope of the seating, spacing, screen.

No. VisionViewer is added to your booking page. Your ticketing system keeps the showtimes, the availability and the payment; we only store the shape of your auditoriums. If you already have a seat map, it stays as it is: you add a button to it.

One tag loads the SDK, then one call is enough. With your own seat map, VisionViewer.open() opens the 3D view on the seats the moviegoer has just picked. Without one, VisionViewer.mountPlan() inserts ours in your page, and the 3D view opens from it.

The integration guide (in French) gives the code, with examples you can run in place.
