Terms of sale

The terms under which VisionViewer provides its service to a cinema operator. They are intended for business customers.

This English version is a courtesy translation. Only the French version is binding.

1. Purpose and contract documents

These terms govern the provision of the VisionViewer service by {{societe.denomination}} (“VisionViewer”) to a business customer (“the Customer”). They do not apply to consumers.

The contract consists of the quote accepted by the Customer, these terms, the service level agreement and the data processing agreement. In the event of a contradiction, the quote prevails, then these terms. The Customer’s purchasing terms apply only if VisionViewer has accepted them in writing.

2. The service

VisionViewer shows each moviegoer the view from their seat, in 3D, within the Customer’s booking flow. Depending on the quote, the service includes:

  • the modeling of the Customer’s auditoriums, from its plans, its photos or an on-site survey;
  • the 3D view, opened from the Customer’s seat map;
  • the 2D seat map, inserted into the Customer’s page when it does not have its own;
  • the integration file (SDK), the guide and the keys that connect the Customer’s website to the service;
  • usage statistics.

VisionViewer may change the service, without reducing its essential functions during the term of the contract.

3. What the service does not do

  • The ticketing system remains the Customer’s. VisionViewer knows neither the showtimes, nor the availability, nor the sales, nor the payments. The seats sold are reported to it by the Customer’s website, and it is the Customer’s ticketing system that decides at payment.
  • The 3D view is a reconstruction. It is computed from the measurements of the auditorium and gives a faithful representation of the view, without constituting a guarantee for the moviegoer. It is up to the Customer to present it as such to its own customers.
  • The statistics are indicative. They rely in part on what the Customer’s website reports; they measure usage and are not used for invoicing.

4. Order and service activation

The order is firm upon acceptance of the quote. VisionViewer then models the auditoriums, then gives the Customer a test key to fine-tune its integration, and a live key for going live. The lead times announced assume that the Customer provides the requested items on time.

5. The Customer’s commitments

  • to provide accurate plans and measurements, and to report any change to an auditorium;
  • to declare the domains of its websites, and to use its keys only on them;
  • to integrate the service according to the guide, and to send the actual status of the seats sold;
  • to hold the rights to the logos and content that it entrusts to VisionViewer;
  • to inform its own users, in its privacy policy, of the use of VisionViewer.

6. Prices

The prices are those of the quote, stated in euros excluding taxes, with VAT added at the rate in force. The travel costs of an on-site survey are stated in the quote.

7. Invoicing and payment

Unless the quote states otherwise, invoices are payable within thirty days of their date of issue, by bank transfer. No discount is granted for early payment.

Any amount not paid when due automatically bears interest at three times the statutory interest rate, and gives rise to a fixed sum of 40 euros as compensation for recovery costs, without prejudice to additional compensation on presentation of supporting documents. After a formal notice that has remained without effect for fifteen days, VisionViewer may suspend the service until payment.

8. Term and termination

The contract is entered into for the term stated in the quote. Failing that, it is entered into for twelve months from service activation, and renews for periods of twelve months, unless one of the parties gives notice of non-renewal two months before the end of the term.

Either party may terminate the contract if the other seriously breaches its obligations and does not remedy the breach within thirty days of a formal notice.

At the end of the contract, the Customer’s keys are deactivated. At its request, VisionViewer gives it the data of its auditoriums in a readable format, then deletes that data, under the terms of the data processing agreement.

9. Service level and support

The target availability, the support and their conditions are described in the service level agreement. They apply to live keys.

10. Intellectual property

VisionViewer remains the holder of all rights in the service, its code, its 3D engine and its documentation. During the term of the contract, the Customer receives the non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use it on its websites and applications.

The Customer remains the owner of its plans, measurements, logos and content. It authorizes VisionViewer to use them to provide the service, and for that purpose only.

VisionViewer does not name the Customer among its references without its written consent.

11. Personal data

For the display of the service on the Customer’s website, VisionViewer acts as the Customer’s processor, under the terms of the data processing agreement. For the management of the business relationship, each party is the controller of its own processing: see the privacy policy.

12. Confidentiality

Each party keeps confidential the non-public information received from the other, uses it only for the performance of the contract, and maintains this obligation for three years after the contract ends.

13. Liability

VisionViewer is bound by a best-efforts obligation. It is not liable for indirect damage, such as a loss of revenue, of customers or of data, nor for the consequences of an inaccurate plan or measurement provided by the Customer.

Its liability, all causes combined, is limited to the amounts paid by the Customer for the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the liability. This limit applies neither to gross negligence, nor to willful misconduct, nor to bodily injury.

14. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for a breach due to a force majeure event within the meaning of Article 1218 of the French Civil Code. If the impediment lasts more than thirty days, either party may terminate the contract without compensation.

15. Subcontracting and assignment

VisionViewer may use providers to host the service; it remains responsible for their work. Neither party assigns the contract without the written consent of the other, except to a company that succeeds it in its business.

16. Governing law and disputes

The contract is governed by French law. The parties first seek an amicable solution. Failing agreement within thirty days, any dispute falls within the jurisdiction of the competent courts of the district in which {{societe.denomination}} has its registered office.
