Online booking and seat choice in French cinemas: the numbers

How many moviegoers book their seat, by what means, and why? How many cinemas sell online, and how many let you choose your seat? Two studies by the CNC, France’s national film agency, published in 2025 and 2026, answer for France.

Four moviegoers in ten book their seat

In 2026, 41.7% of moviegoers booked their cinema seat in advance, up from 35.3% in 2025. The practice has almost tripled in ten years: 14.4% in 2016, 25.6% in 2019, before the pandemic.

The youngest book the most. Among 25–34-year-olds, 59.1% book; among 15–24-year-olds, 49.7%; among seniors, 28.6%. Among frequent moviegoers, booking is the majority practice: 54.1%.

The phone first

Among moviegoers who book, 46.4% do so on their smartphone, 40.9% on a computer, and 11% by stopping at the cinema beforehand. In 2016, the smartphone was a small minority: its share has gained 32.9 points since.

For a cinema, the consequence is direct: the booking flow is judged first on a small screen, held in one hand.

Why they book

  • 59.7% to be sure of having a seat for the film and the showtime of their choice.
  • 43.9% to avoid the line.
  • 22.4% to be able to choose their seat. Among seniors, 28.6%.

Choosing the seat therefore comes third. That figure has to be read with the next one: a large share of cinemas do not offer it.

Seven cinemas in ten sell online

In 2025, 68.9% of cinemas sell tickets online, up from 58.4% in 2021 and 47.9% in 2018. The gap between cinemas remains wide:

Sales go first through the cinema’s own website: that is the case for 94.6% of cinemas that sell online. The listings site Allociné comes far behind, at 33.7%.

Seat choice, almost reserved for chains

Among cinemas that sell online, 95.7% of those belonging to a chain let moviegoers choose their seat, 23.6 points more than in 2021. Outside chains, 5.1% do, and that figure has not progressed.

The CNC sums it up in one phrase: seat choice is the preserve of the chains. A moviegoer who wants to choose their seat can therefore, most often, only do so in a chain cinema.

What share of sales goes online?

The CNC does not publish the share of tickets sold online in France. It asked exhibitors who sell online to estimate theirs:

A third of chain cinemas therefore estimate that they already sell more than half of their tickets online. By way of comparison, in Germany, 51% of tickets were bought online in 2025, according to the federal film board, the FFA.

The cinema estate, to put these figures in context

In 2025, mainland France has 2,060 active cinemas and 6,401 screens, for 1.13 million seats. That makes, on average, 180 seats per auditorium.

What these figures do not say

  • They are statements. Moviegoers describe their last outing; exhibitors estimate their sales.
  • Booking is not buying online. 11% of those who book do so at the cinema.
  • Seat choice is only measured among cinemas that sell online. The CNC does not say what share of showtimes or auditoriums has numbered seats.
  • The survey of exhibitors under-represents single-screen cinemas, according to the CNC itself.
  • None of these studies measures the effect of a view from the seat. We know of none for cinemas. They establish that booking is growing and that seat choice matters; they do not say what seeing the seat changes.

Frequently asked questions

What share of French moviegoers book their cinema seat in advance?

41.7% in 2026, up from 35.3% in 2025 and 25.6% in 2019, according to the CNC study on the cinema-going practices of the French. Among them, 46.4% book on a smartphone and 40.9% on a computer.

How many cinemas sell tickets online in France?

68.9% of cinemas in 2025, according to the CNC survey of exhibitors: all chains and multiplexes, 53.8% of cinemas outside chains and 37.6% of single-screen cinemas.

How many French cinemas let you choose your seat online?

Among cinemas that sell online, 95.7% of those belonging to a chain offer seat choice, compared with 5.1% of cinemas outside chains, according to the CNC’s 2025 survey of exhibitors.

How many cinemas and screens are there in France?

2,060 active cinemas and 6,401 screens in mainland France in 2025, for 1.13 million seats, according to the CNC’s Géographie du cinéma.
