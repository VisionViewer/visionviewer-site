Privacy and cookies

VisionViewer shows an auditorium, not a moviegoer. We create no account, we track no one, and nothing is written to the browser of the person viewing an auditorium. Here is, precisely, the little that we process.

What we do not collect

  • No moviegoer account: no name, no email address, no identifier of the person viewing an auditorium.
  • No audience measurement and no advertising: no third-party analytics tool, no advertising tracker, on any of our websites.
  • No ticketing: no showtimes, no availability, no sales, no payments. All of that stays with the cinema.
  • Nothing in the moviegoer’s browser: the 3D view and the seat map write neither a cookie nor local storage there.

On a cinema’s website

A cinema that integrates VisionViewer displays, on its booking page, a 3D view and sometimes a seat map served by app.visionviewer.net. As with any resource loaded on the web, the visitor’s browser then sends our hosting provider its IP address and the name of the browser: this is the only personal information we receive.

Our statistics are counts per day, per cinema and per auditorium: seat maps displayed, 3D views opened, seats selected, orders reported by the cinema’s website. None of these counters relates to a person. Of an order reference that the cinema chooses to send us, we keep only a hash, not the reference itself, so as not to count the same order twice.

The seats chosen by the visitor travel between the cinema’s page and our pages, in the visitor’s browser. We do not link them to anyone.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, its rectification, its erasure, the restriction of its processing, its portability, and you can object to its processing. You can also tell us what should become of it after your death.

Write to {{contact}}: we reply within one month. If the request concerns your visit to a cinema’s website, contact that cinema first, as it is the controller of that processing; we help it reply to you.

If our reply does not satisfy you, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (Commission nationale de l’informatique et des libertés), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, or at cnil.fr.

Security

  • All exchanges go through an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • The 3D view and the seat map are displayed only on the websites declared for the cinema’s key, and accept messages only from the page that embeds them.
  • A key can be revoked at any time.
  • The back office is accessible only to VisionViewer administrators, through a time-limited session.
